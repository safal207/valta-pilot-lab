Privacy note

Minimal data, one clear purpose.

The opt-in form asks for a work email, an optional company name, and the workflow transition that becomes uncertain after timeout. This information is used to deliver the requested kit, send no more than three related educational follow-ups, and understand whether the material is relevant to a bounded payment-recovery workflow.

What is not requested

Do not submit payment credentials, API keys, wallet keys, personal transaction data, customer records, production evidence, confidential architecture, or regulated financial data.

Current submission path

Until a dedicated consent-aware form provider is connected, the page opens a prefilled email in the visitor’s own email application. The static site itself does not store the form data. When a provider is connected, its endpoint and privacy terms must be documented before activation.

Sharing and retention

Contact data is not sold or transferred as a mailing list. A distribution partner may receive aggregate attribution results, but not subscriber addresses, unless the subscriber separately and explicitly requests an introduction.

Unsubscribe or deletion

Reply to any delivery email with “unsubscribe” or request deletion of the submitted contact data.
