THE PROOF BREAKS BETWEEN SYSTEMS
Cross-System Payment Recovery — Public Proof Card

EXPENSIVE STATE
Payment action:   authorized and dispatched
Provider:         ACCEPTED or response lost
External rail:    unknown or delayed
Customer ledger:  unknown or unreconciled
Automation:       requests retry

API accepted
!= recipient credited
!= settlement final
!= ledger reconciled
!= safe to retry

FOUR HONEST VERDICTS
VERIFIED
One matching external effect and linked ledger result are observed.
Finalize; do not retry.

SAFE_TO_RETRY
An explicit pre-effect rejection and zero external effects are proven.
A new attempt may be permitted under policy.

UNVERIFIED
The provider claims acceptance, but the expected external effect is absent.
Do not claim success.

RECONCILE_REQUIRED
An effect may have occurred, or evidence domains disagree.
Block blind retry until reconciliation.

EXECUTABLE SANDBOX RESULT
24 retry attempts
1 durable reservation owner
23 stale or duplicate attempts rejected
1 external rail effect
1 linked customer-ledger posting
receipt verification: PASS

WHY BOTH PROOFS MATTER
Concurrency control proves which attempt owned dispatch.
Independent observation proves what happened outside that boundary.
